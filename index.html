About Mikail

D. J. Mikail Maillet is a seasoned Martial Artist with nearly 50 years of experience, holding certifications across multiple disciplines. His intellectual journey spans over 45 years, during which he has deeply explored both Eastern and Western philosophies, psychology, and diverse global faiths. A dedicated seeker of knowledge, he is committed to rigorous self experimentation to test the validity of the theories he encounters.

As a life-long meditator and practitioner of various arts, his passion for understanding is unwavering. He resides minutes from the picturesque town of Harrison Hot Springs with his beloved wife, just minutes away from one of British Columbia's most stunning lakes, a place that continues to inspire him.
